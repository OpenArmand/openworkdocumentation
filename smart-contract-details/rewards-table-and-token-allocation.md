# Rewards Table (And Token Allocation)

**60%: Community**

Earns through actually giving or doing jobs on OpenWork and unlocking tokens through governance\
\
**20%:  Team & Contributors**&#x20;

Unlocked after 10,000 governance actions completed, necessary for building OpenWork, initial governance and guarantees functionality needed for decentralised dispute resolution\
\
**20%: Investors**

5% pre-seed token sale, Investor tokens have 1 year cliff, 3 year vesting. 15% reserved for future fundraises.&#x20;
