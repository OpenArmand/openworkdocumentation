# Earnings and Reward Contract

Earnings and Reward Contract manages how tokens are earned, redeemed, and allocated to participants based on their work and contributions.

| Feature                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | Description                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Earnings & Governance Rewards are given at the completion of each job to the Job Giver & Referrers based on the ratios related to the total job value on OpenWork. Note that pricing of the token would need to be gotten from a source like a Uniswap pool \[potentially it could be self determined but this could be dangerous and needs to be explored]. | <p>Struct :<br>Earned Tokens<br>{<br>[address:tokens,...]</p><p>}<br><br>Every time someone releases a payment in the job contracts:<br>updateRewards(jobID){<br>getJobGiver(jobID)<br>getReferrer(jobGiver_address)<br>getJobTaker(jobID)<br>getReferrer(jobTaker_address)<br>updateEarnedToken(jobGiver_address, jobTaker_adress)<br>}</p>                                                            |
| <p>Tokens are issued upon job completion based on cumulative platform value:<br><br>&#x3C; $1M: 100% of job value<br><br>$1M–$10M: 50% of job value<br><br>$10M–$100M: 25% of job value<br><br>$100M–$1B: 10% of job value</p>                                                                                                                               | <p>The total job value can be fetched from the Native OpenWork Contract to determine the right amount of rewards to be distributed.<br><br>The token price in relation to USD will be fetched from a uniswap pool. <br>To create this pool, we just need to go to the uniswap and create a pool, get the link to the pool and link it on our contract so that it can fetch the price of the token. </p> |
| If a referrer exists, 10% of tokens go to the referrer and 90% to the job giver; otherwise, the job giver receives 100%.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| A job taker’s referrer also earns 10% of the fee                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| People with earned tokens can redeem 10k tokens per governance action.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Tokens are claimable once staking periods are done, or can be re-staked                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | An earner can call the claimRewards function after they have completed the necessary number of Governance actions for the amount of tokens they are trying to claim. The tokens will be transferred to their wallet after 1 month of safety period.                                                                                                                                                     |
| Further, job givers can allocate a percentage of some tokens to the job takers based on their agreements with them \[of the pool given to them]                                                                                                                                                                                                              |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |



| Type of Token           | Stake Period                                     | Pre-Staked? | Governance Action Required                       | Governance Power                        |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | ----------- | ------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------- |
| Earned Tokens           | 1 year (pre-staked each time they're earned)     | Yes         | Yes (e.g., 1 action per 10,000 tokens to unlock) | 1x                                      |
| Team Tokens             | 1/3 for 1 year, 1/3 for 2 years, 1/3 for 3 years | Yes         | Yes (e.g., 1 action per 10,000 tokens)           | 1x, 2x, or 3x based on staking duration |
| Voluntary Staked Tokens | 1, 2, or 3 years (user choice)                   | No          | No                                               | 1x, 2x, or 3x based on duration         |

The Earnings and Reward Contract on the OpenWork Chain will calculate and store the rewards for all users on the OW chain but the rewards will be claimed through the Main DAO contract on Ethereum after the necessary governance is done to redeem the tokens after the staking period.\
