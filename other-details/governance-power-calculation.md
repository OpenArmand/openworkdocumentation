# Governance Power Calculation

| Type of Token           | Stake Period                                                                         | Pre-Staked? | Governance Action Required                                                                 | Governance Power                |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------- |
| Earned Tokens           | see [Rewards Table](../smart-contract-details/rewards-table-and-token-allocation.md) | Yes         | Yes (see [Rewards Table](../smart-contract-details/rewards-table-and-token-allocation.md)) | 1x                              |
| Voluntary Staked Tokens | 1, 2, or 3 years (user choice)                                                       | No          | No                                                                                         | 1x, 2x, or 3x based on duration |
