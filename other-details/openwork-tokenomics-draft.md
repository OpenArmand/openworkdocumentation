# OpenWork Tokenomics Draft

Principles of OpenWork Tokenomics:\
\
1\) The token's purpose is governance, not trading. Hence almost all tokens held or earned require governance to be redeemed.&#x20;

2\) The tokens must be given mainly to the people who earn it through jobs being given on OpenWork, as they understand the model best and this decentralises governance long term. Higher incentives are needed initially, then as market making starts, it can be lower.\


1\. Token Distribution for 1 Billion OpenWork Tokens

The 1 billion OpenWork tokens are allocated across various activities and participants to ensure a balanced distribution that fosters governance participation, rewards active contributors, and provides liquidity for platform operations.

\- Earnings & Governance Rewards: 300 million tokens\
\- Team & Contributors: 200 million tokens\
\- Ecosystem Growth: 100 million tokens\
\- Private Round: 50 million tokens

\- ICO: 200 million tokens\
\- Reserve & Liquidity: 150 million tokens (controlled by DAO in treasury)



2\. Tokens Issued Based on Transaction Value Ranges

Earnings & Governance Rewards are tokens given directly to people who use OpenWork and then need to perform governance actions like decentralised dispute resolution to redeem them. So for example if less than $1m in job value is added to OpenWork and if I give a job for $10k, I will get $10k worth of tokens on OpenWork. If more than $10M in job value has already been added, and I give a job for $10k, I will get $2.5k worth of tokens on OpenWork.

If there is a referrer for a job giver, the referrer would earn 10% of the amount, leaving 90% to the job giver. If no referrer exists, it goes directly to the job giver. Similarly, for the job taker end, if there is a referrer for the job taker, they’d get 10% of the fee for referring him.

Note that it is possible that tokens marked redeemable can be taken back if found that the job was fraudulent.

3\. Governance Mechanism for Token Redemption

To ensure that token holders actively contribute to the platform's decision-making, OpenWork requires participants to complete governance actions proportional to their token holdings. Each holder must perform one governance action per 10,000 tokens received within a one-year lock period to redeem them.

If a user receives 1 million tokens, they must complete 100 governance actions in that year to retain and redeem their tokens. Unredeemed tokens will return to the Earning & Government Rewards pool.&#x20;

View the [probable scenario](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1qzZ6mbucCJXaGdvabvwaGHc5qbz8VWnfjuEs8BOszEA/edit?usp=sharing) for when the token would be released and how. Note that governance includes skill oracle related governance.\


3\. Team

The team tokens also are treated as equal to the Earnings & Governance rewards tokens, in terms of how they are redeemed through active governance. But instead of all getting released in 1 year, after a 1 year cliff, approximately 4.17% of the tokens will be unlocked each month over the following 24 months subject to that member’s contribution as per promise.

For contributors, custom vested token contracts depending on the deal should be given to them using a tool that exists for the same.

We can consider larger token locks for the team, on further discussion.

The 100M ecosystem growth pool is used to incentivise growth and adoption of openwork through partnerships, grants, or incentivising developers building on top of openwork, like programmable work applications.

&#x20;

4\. Staked Tokens&#x20;

Staked Tokens are treated the same as Earnings & Governance rewards tokens, but can be unstaked without the need to perform the governance functions necessarily.



4\. Private Round&#x20;

The token can be valued at just $5M in the private round, so 5% results in $250k raised for OpenWork to help us get traction needed before the ICO.

To minimize market volatility before the project matures, tokens will be subject to a three-year vesting schedule: after a 1 year cliff, approximately 4.17% of the tokens will be unlocked each month over the following 24 months.



4\. ICO

We can launch this once there are at least $200k worth of transactions.

The token can be valued at just $10M in the ICO, so 20% results in $2M raised for OpenWork.

This can include private investors too.

To minimize market volatility before the project matures, tokens will be subject to a three-year vesting schedule: after a 1 year cliff, approximately 4.17% of the tokens will be unlocked each month over the following 24 months.
